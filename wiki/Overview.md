# The MAGA New Right: Origins, Philosophy, and Transformation

*This page is the master synthesis of the wiki. It will grow with every source ingested.*

---

## The Central Question

How did the Republican Party and American conservative movement transform into the MAGA New Right? What are its philosophical roots, who are its key thinkers, and what does it actually stand for?

This wiki traces that transformation — from the postwar conservative consensus through the fractures that produced something new: a movement that is nationalist, anti-liberal, anti-administrative, and in many ways hostile to the constitutional order it claims to defend.
