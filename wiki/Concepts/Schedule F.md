# Schedule F

**Executive order mechanism — first signed by Trump (October 2020), revoked by Biden (January 2021), re-signed by Trump (January 2025) — that reclassifies federal civil servants in "policy-related" positions as at-will "Schedule F" employees, removing their civil service job protections and enabling mass dismissal and replacement with political loyalists**

Schedule F is the single most structurally significant provision in [[Project 2025]]'s governing blueprint. It translates the New Right's theoretical critique of the [[MAGA New Right|administrative state]] into a concrete mechanism for personnel capture.

Under normal civil service rules, federal employees have strong protections against politically motivated dismissal. Schedule F strips those protections from any employee whose work "influences policy" — an extremely broad category that could encompass tens of thousands of positions across all executive agencies.

The practical effect: a second Trump administration could fire career civil servants who are perceived as disloyal and replace them with ideologically vetted political appointees, dramatically increasing presidential control over the federal bureaucracy.

This connects directly to the New Right's theoretical commitments:
- [[Adrian Vermeule]]'s "[[Integration from Within]]" strategy explicitly calls for infiltrating and recapturing administrative institutions
- [[Longmarcherism]] frames this as the right's counter-march through institutions
- The [[Claremont Institute]]'s constitutional theory (via [[John Eastman]] and others) holds that Article II gives the president near-unlimited control over executive branch personnel
