# Great Replacement Theory

**The conspiracist claim that white populations in Western countries are being deliberately demographically displaced by non-white immigrants, often at the alleged direction of elites; the bridge concept linking mainstream New Right immigration politics to Hard Right white nationalism**

Originated in French nativist Renaud Camus's *Le Grand Remplacement* (2011) and circulated through European far-right and American white-nationalist networks before crossing into mainstream conservative discourse. The "softer" demographic-anxiety version — that immigration is changing electoral coalitions and cultural identity — has been mainstreamed by [[Tucker Carlson]] (especially in *The End of Men* and his Fox News monologues) and is implicit in much New Right rhetoric on the border.

The theory is a primary load-bearing wall between the New Right proper and the [[Hard Right]] (Spencer, BAP, neoreactionaries), where it appears in its explicit white-nationalist form. Mainstream New Right figures generally avoid the phrase but recycle the underlying demographic anxiety.
