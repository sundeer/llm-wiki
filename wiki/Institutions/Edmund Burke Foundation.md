# Edmund Burke Foundation

**Washington D.C.-based think tank founded by [[Yoram Hazony]] after the success of *The Virtue of Nationalism* (2018); primary organizer of the National Conservatism conference series (NatCon 1–4, 2019–2024); principal institutional vehicle for National Conservatism as a formal intellectual movement**

## Key Figures

- **[[Yoram Hazony]]** — chairman; intellectual architect of National Conservatism
- **[[David Brog]]** — president; Princeton BA, Harvard Law JD; formerly executive director of Christians United for Israel
- **[[Christopher DeMuth]]** — conference chairman; AEI president 1986–2008; gave the organization mainstream conservative credibility

## Role

The Edmund Burke Foundation does not produce academic research in the traditional sense; its primary function is convening. The NatCon conferences it organizes are the major venue where thinkers, politicians, journalists, and activists from the nationalist right's various factions come together. NatCon 4 (July 2024, Washington D.C.) marked the movement's arrival in the Republican mainstream, with JD Vance and other senior politicians on the program.

Field notes that the Foundation's proximity to the Hard Right — via Hazony's appearance at [[Paul Gottfried]]'s H.L. Mencken Club — raises questions about the movement's willingness to draw meaningful lines between National Conservatism and white nationalism.

[[Nathan Pinkoski]] was brought on as a senior fellow following his controversial NatCon 3 talk (2022).
