# Claremont Institute

**Conservative think tank; Southern California; founded 1979; intellectual home of [[The Claremonters]]**

---

## What It Is

The Claremont Institute is the institutional center of the Claremonter camp of the [[MAGA New Right]]. Founded in 1979 by students of [[Harry V. Jaffa]], it is unlike most Washington think tanks: it has been "far less policy-oriented and wonkish" and "more overtly political." Its political bent comes entirely from Jaffa's thinking.

The Institute has no formal connection to the Claremont group of colleges in California, though there is overlap among faculty and scholars.

- **The American Mind** — the Institute's online publication; published [[The Fight is Now]] on November 4, 2020

## History and Role

The Institute was founded by Jaffa's students following the Goldwater movement, keeping alive the West Coast Straussian tradition through the Reagan era and beyond. It operated in relative obscurity until the 2016 election, when [[Michael Anton]]'s [[The Flight 93 Election]] — published in the *Claremont Review of Books* — went viral and made the Institute a central node of the intellectual MAGA movement.

Rush Limbaugh's September 7, 2016 show, in which he read the essay to ~15 million listeners and called it "a home run, every paragraph," transformed the Institute's profile overnight.

---

## The Institute and January 6

The Claremont Institute's role in the "Stop the Steal" movement was substantial:

- [[The Fight is Now]] (November 4, 2020) called for huge rallies and legal battles immediately after the election, before any credible fraud claims had been investigated
- John Eastman authored the "Coup Memos" providing legal cover for Pence to refuse to certify the 2020 electoral results
- [[Michael Anton]] published "Game-on for the Coup?" — linked approvingly in *The Fight is Now*

Field: the Institute's institutional imprimatur gave the stolen-election narrative intellectual legitimacy within conservative circles.

---

## Relationship to Hillsdale College

While formally separate, Hillsdale College in Michigan operates as a closely affiliated institution. Larry P. Arnn (Claremont Graduate University PhD, Jaffa student) became its president. Hillsdale's free online Constitution courses have reached millions of Americans, extending the Claremonter influence far beyond elite circles.
