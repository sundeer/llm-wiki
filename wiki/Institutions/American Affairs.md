# American Affairs

**Journal; founded 2017; co-founded by Julius Krein ("Plautus") and [[Gladden Pappin]]; published [[Integration from Within]] by [[Adrian Vermeule]]**

---

## What It Is

*American Affairs* is a quarterly journal that emerged from the intellectual milieu of the [[Journal of American Greatness]] — the anonymous blog that developed early Trumpist arguments in 2016. It was co-founded by Julius Krein (who wrote for JAG under the pseudonym "Plautus") and [[Gladden Pappin]].

The journal occupies an unusual space: it takes seriously the economic critique of neoliberalism (publishing pieces on industrial policy, labor economics, and market concentration) while also serving as a major venue for [[Postliberalism|Postliberal]] and New Right ideas. It has been associated with what some call "Trumpist political economy."

---

## Significance

*American Affairs* published [[Adrian Vermeule]]'s landmark essay [[Integration from Within]] in its inaugural year (Spring 2018), which became one of the founding documents of practical Catholic integralism. The journal's combination of serious economic heterodoxy with postliberal politics made it distinctive in the New Right intellectual ecosystem.
