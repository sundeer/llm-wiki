# American Greatness

**Online political magazine and website that grew directly out of the anonymous [[Journal of American Greatness]] blog (2016); funded in part by [[Peter Thiel]]; publishes Trumpist and New Right commentary, analysis, and polemics; part of the Hard Right media ecosystem alongside [[IM-1776]], *American Mind*, *Revolver News*, and *TheBlaze***

American Greatness (AmGreatness.com) is the institutionalized successor to the Journal of American Greatness, the anonymous 2016 blog that first developed a sophisticated intellectual defense of Trumpism. Where JAG was a temporary intellectual experiment, American Greatness became a permanent outlet for pro-Trump commentary and New Right analysis.

Christopher Flannery, one of the original Claremont Institute founders, wrote in American Greatness (August 2022): "John Eastman Is an American Hero: He Put His Life on the Line to Stop an Unconstitutional Election" — an example of how the publication functions as a venue for Claremont-adjacent positions that are too extreme for the *Claremont Review of Books*.
