# Witherspoon Institute

**Princeton-adjacent conservative research institute founded 2003; closely tied to [[Robert P. George]]'s [[James Madison Program]] orbit; major node for natural-law jurisprudence, integralist-curious legal scholarship, and the social-conservative intellectual establishment that the New Right both inherits from and reacts against**

Witherspoon publishes *Public Discourse* and has incubated much of the academic infrastructure linking traditional Catholic natural-law thought, originalist legal theory, and the postliberal turn. Figures associated with the Witherspoon/George ecosystem — Hadley Arkes, Princeton-trained legal scholars, [[Adrian Vermeule]] (in his earlier originalist phase) — represent the older fusionist conservative-legal world that the New Right is partly extending and partly disowning.

The Witherspoon network helped seed [[Declarationism]]'s academic legitimacy via the Bradford-Jaffa debate genealogy ([[Beyond Originalism]]).
