# James Madison Program

**Princeton's "James Madison Program in American Ideals and Institutions"; conservative civics institute founded 2000 and directed by [[Robert P. George]]; postdoctoral pipeline for conservative Catholic intellectuals; venue where [[Adrian Vermeule]] delivered his Schmittian "All Human Conflict Is Ultimately Theological" speech (2019); part of a broader network of George-orbit programs adjacent to elite universities (cf. Tellez/FEHE).**

The Program funds fellowships and events and has hosted a generation of natural-law and originalist scholars while increasingly serving as a host site for postliberal and Schmitt-curious work. [[Nathan Pinkoski]] held a postdoctoral fellowship there before moving to the Zephyr Institute and the [[Edmund Burke Foundation]]. Its institutional position — Princeton respectability paired with George's Catholic conservative network — makes it a key node where mainstream legal academia, [[Catholic Integralism]], and the [[Witherspoon Institute]]/Public Discourse world overlap.
