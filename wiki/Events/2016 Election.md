# 2016 US Presidential Election

**Donald Trump defeats Hillary Clinton; the founding event of the MAGA New Right as a governing movement; the election that [[The Flight 93 Election]] was written to influence; the pivot point in the New Right's long intellectual prehistory**

---

## What Happened

On November 8, 2016, Donald Trump defeated Hillary Clinton in the Electoral College (306–232) while losing the popular vote by approximately 3 million votes. Trump won by flipping Pennsylvania, Michigan, and Wisconsin — "Rust Belt" states that had voted Democratic in presidential elections since the 1980s or longer — by narrow margins.

The result shocked virtually everyone: pollsters, media, the Democratic Party, the Republican establishment, and most of the New Right intellectuals who had been building toward this moment. [[Michael Anton]]'s [[The Flight 93 Election]] — published in September 2016 — had argued that Trump was the only possible candidate who could prevent civilizational catastrophe, but few expected it to work.

---

## Intellectual Significance

The 2016 election is the moment when the New Right's long intellectual project collided with electoral democracy:

**For the Claremonters**: the election vindicated [[The Flight 93 Election]]'s framing — the charge worked, the cockpit was taken. The [[Claremont Institute]] went from a respected but marginal conservative think tank to a central node of the MAGA intellectual infrastructure almost overnight.

**For the Postliberals**: Trump's victory confirmed that liberalism was in crisis and that the time for [[Postliberalism]] had arrived. [[Patrick Deneen]]'s *Why Liberalism Failed* (2018) was already written; the election gave it its context.

**For the Hard Right**: Trump's explicit anti-immigration rhetoric, his praise of authoritarian leaders, and his demolition of the Republican establishment vindicated the Hard Right's prediction that populist nationalism could win. [[Steve Bannon]] moved from Breitbart to the White House.

**For the New Right broadly**: the election killed [[Fusionism]] as the Republican Party's dominant ideology. The Bush-era Republican orthodoxy — free trade, immigration, interventionism — was repudiated by the party's own voters.

---

## The Intellectual Precursors

The 2016 election did not come from nowhere. The intellectual infrastructure that made it possible had been building for decades:
- [[Harry V. Jaffa]]'s [[West Coast Straussianism]] provided the political philosophy
- [[Pat Buchanan]]'s [[Paleoconservatism]] (1990s) provided the template for trade/immigration/anti-interventionism
- The internet Right and [[Hard Right]] figures like [[Curtis Yarvin]] provided the cultural energy and the "red pill" pipeline
- [[The Flight 93 Election]] provided the intellectual permission slip for elite conservatives to vote Trump
