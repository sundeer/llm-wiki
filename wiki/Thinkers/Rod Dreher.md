# Rod Dreher

**Conservative writer and author; formerly at *The American Conservative*; author of *The Benedict Option* (2017); Orthodox Christian convert; known for "NETTR" (No Enemies to the Right) debates; relocated to Hungary 2022**

Dreher coined the "Benedict Option" — a strategy of intentional Christian community withdrawal from mainstream culture, drawing on the Rule of Saint Benedict. [[Patrick Deneen]]'s early prescriptive work (before *Regime Change*) gestured in a similar direction, though Deneen later moved decisively away from withdrawal toward political activism.

Dreher's relocation to Hungary in 2022 tracked his increasing admiration for [[Viktor Orbán]]'s illiberal Christian democratic model, though he later expressed disillusionment.

He figures in the [[IM-1776]] orbit via a controversy over **NETTR** — "No Enemies to the Right" — a Hard Right policy of abandoning the cordon sanitaire against white nationalists. Dreher found NETTR "unhinged and morally corrupt," placing him at odds with the hardest edges of the right-wing media ecosystem.
