# George W. Carey (1933–2013)

**Georgetown University political scientist (1961–2013); student of [[Willmoore Kendall]] at Yale; co-author with Kendall of *The Basic Symbols of the American Political Tradition* (1970, posthumous for Kendall); editor of *The Political Science Reviewer*; the figure who carried the *populist-majoritarian, anti-Lockean* reading of the American founding through the academy from Kendall's generation to the post-2000 paleoconservative inheritors.**

---

## Why He Matters Here

Carey is the principal academic transmitter of [[Willmoore Kendall|Kendall]]'s populist-majoritarian, deliberative-legislative-supremacy reading of the American constitutional order. *The Basic Symbols of the American Political Tradition* (which Carey completed and published after Kendall's 1967 death) is the canonical statement of the position: the American tradition is *Mayflower-Compact Christian-republican*, not *Declaration-of-Independence Lockean-individualist*.

This reading is the *paleoconservative* reading that [[Harry V. Jaffa|Jaffa]]'s [[Declarationism]] was constructed against (see [[Beyond Originalism]]). Without Carey's continuation, the Kendall position would have lapsed; with him, it remained available for the [[M.E. Bradford|Bradford]]-Dallas-paleoconservative line and, indirectly, for [[Patrick Deneen|Deneen]]'s Notre Dame anti-Lockean Catholic political theory.
