# Matthew Dallek

**Political historian; professor at George Washington University's Graduate School of Political Management; author of *Birchers: How the John Birch Society Radicalized the American Right* (Basic Books, 2023). The wiki cites Dallek as the principal source for the revisionist account of the [[John Birch Society]] episode and the [[William F. Buckley Jr.|Buckley]] "purge" myth — see [[Debunking a Longstanding Myth About William F. Buckley]] (the *Politico* book excerpt that is currently the wiki's primary engagement with his work).**

Dallek's earlier book *The Right Moment: Ronald Reagan's First Victory and the Decisive Turning Point in American Politics* is also referenced in the wiki's [[Ronald Reagan]] page for its account of Reagan's pre-1965 ambivalence about the JBS.

Dallek is a historian, not a movement figure; his role here is methodological. His revisionism — drawing on Daniel Schlozman, Sam Rosenfeld, and his own archival work in *NR* correspondence — is a load-bearing input to the wiki's [[Counterrevolution]] thesis. Stub for now; expand if more of his work is ingested.
