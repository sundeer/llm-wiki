# Matthew Spalding

**Vice President of [[Hillsdale College]]'s Washington operations and Dean of the Van Andel Graduate School of Government; long-tenured [[Heritage Foundation]] scholar before joining Hillsdale; executive director of the 1776 Commission; a key institutional connector between Hillsdale, Heritage, and the Trump-era originalist project**

Spalding worked at Heritage for two decades on the founders, the Constitution, and civic education before moving to Hillsdale. He served as executive director of Trump's [[1776 Commission Report]] and is one of the principal "1776"/American-founding-as-civic-religion figures in the Hillsdale-Claremont orbit. He departed Heritage during Kay Cole James's tenure along with David Azerrad, Arthur Milikh, and Ryan T. Anderson — the cohort whose exits flagged Heritage's coming radicalization under [[Kevin Roberts]].
