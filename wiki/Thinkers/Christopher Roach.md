# Christopher Roach

**American conservative writer and attorney; regular contributor to *American Greatness*; author of "The Salazar Option" (*American Greatness*, 2021) — the wiki's named touchstone for the contemporary American conservative argument that [[António Salazar|Salazar]]'s *Estado Novo* is a model for "use of State power at the service of a counterrevolution" against revolutionary leftism.**

---

## Why He Matters Here

Per [[Iberian Vistas]], Roach's "The Salazar Option" enumerates the *Estado Novo*'s lessons for the American Right:

- Implement "systemic change" rather than incremental reform
- Take off the "kid gloves" when dealing with hostile and revolutionary elements
- Reject the [[Rod Dreher|Dreher]] "Benedict option" of cultural retreat
- Become "willing to use power in order to survive and win"

Roach's argument is the cleanest single statement, in mainstream-conservative venue, of the Salazar-as-operational-model thesis. It is published in *American Greatness*, the [[Claremont Institute]]-adjacent journal that the wiki tracks as a central institutional venue for the [[MAGA New Right]].

The structural significance of "The Salazar Option" is its translation of Salazar from *historical figure* to *strategic instrument*. Where mid-century *NR* admiration of Salazar was retrospective ("Salazar governs Portugal well"), Roach's framing is prospective ("here is what Salazar's regime did that the American right should emulate").
