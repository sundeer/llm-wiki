# Laura K. Field

**Liberal political theorist; senior fellow at the Niskanen Center and at the Bulwark; author of *[[Furious Minds]]: Political Theory in the Age of Trump* (Princeton University Press, 2025); the anchor author for this wiki**

Field is a political theorist whose work focuses on how intellectual life intersects with political movements. *Furious Minds* is a sustained examination of the thinkers and ideas animating the MAGA New Right — covering the [[The Claremonters|Claremonters]], [[National Conservatism]], [[Postliberalism]], and the [[Hard Right]] underbelly connecting them.

Her prior academic work includes research on conservative campus culture ("[[On Campus Trump Fans Safe Spaces]]") and related questions about the politics of higher education.

Field's approach in *Furious Minds* is unusual: she takes the New Right's intellectual claims seriously enough to engage them on their own terms while ultimately arguing that the movement represents a betrayal of the democratic commitments its thinkers claim to defend.
