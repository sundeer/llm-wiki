# Michael Knowles (b. 1990)

**American conservative podcaster and Daily Wire host; Yale undergraduate (2012); Catholic; host of *The Michael Knowles Show*; the wiki's named contemporary popular-conservative voice expressing positive view of [[Francisco Franco|Franco]] ("given the circumstances… Franco was pretty good," 2023).**

---

## Why He Matters Here

Per [[Iberian Vistas]], Knowles is one of the contemporary popular-conservative voices that has — without academic qualification or apologetic hedging — defended Franco on a major commercial conservative platform. The wiki's reading: Knowles's Franco defense is structurally similar to [[Joseph Pearce|Pearce]]'s but reaches a substantially larger and younger audience via Daily Wire. The presence of Franco apologetics on Daily Wire (a flagship mainstream-conservative outlet rather than a *First Things*-style intellectual publication) is the wiki's marker that the Iberian revival is not confined to elite intellectual venues.

Knowles is also relevant for the wiki's [[Christian Nationalism]] track. His March 2023 CPAC speech declaring that "transgenderism must be eradicated from public life entirely" was the cleanest single statement in mainstream-conservative venue of the eliminationist register the wiki tracks under Christian Nationalism.
