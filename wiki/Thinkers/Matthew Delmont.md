# Matthew Delmont

**Dartmouth Sherman Fairchild Distinguished Professor in History; historian of African-American politics, civil rights, and 20th-century race; author of *Half American: The Epic Story of African Americans Fighting World War II at Home and Abroad* (2022); the wiki cites him as the author of [[How Jackie Robinson Helped Defeat a Trump-Like Candidate]] (*Atlantic*, March 2016), which drew the explicit Goldwater-Trump line via Black Republican memory in real time during the 2016 primary.**
