# Jim DeMint

**Republican Senator from South Carolina (2005–2013); Tea Party leader; president of the [[Heritage Foundation]] (2013–2017); the figure who first turned Heritage from a Reaganite policy shop into an activist conservative organization, paving the way for [[Kevin Roberts]]'s full MAGA conversion**

DeMint's tenure was contentious internally — one trustee told the press, "DeMint has not only politicized Heritage, he's also trivialized it." Under his leadership Heritage Action (the 501(c)(4) advocacy arm) became a tool for whipping House Republicans against insufficiently conservative legislation, fueling the Freedom Caucus dynamic. DeMint was forced out in 2017 and replaced by Kay Cole James, who attempted a "return to normalcy" before [[Kevin Roberts]] took over in October 2021 and went much further than DeMint ever did.
