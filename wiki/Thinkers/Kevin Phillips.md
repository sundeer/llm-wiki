# Kevin Phillips (1940–2023)

**Republican political strategist and historian; author of *The Emerging Republican Majority* (1969) — the canonical formalization of [[Barry Goldwater|Goldwater]]'s 1964 Deep South coalition as a viable national strategy; the strategist most directly responsible for translating the 1964 electoral map into Nixon's 1968 Southern Strategy and beyond.**

---

## Why He Matters Here

Per [[He Knew He Was Right|Menand 2001]], *The Emerging Republican Majority* is the *first* explicit paradigm formalizing what Goldwater's 1964 sweep of the Deep South had revealed: that a Republican majority could be constructed by combining the traditional GOP suburban base with realigning Southern white Democrats. The book was published a year after Nixon's election and supplied the analytic vocabulary the post-1968 GOP used to interpret its coalition.

Phillips is wiki-relevant as the figure who moved the [[1964 Republican National Convention|1964]] insurgency's *electoral lesson* into the GOP establishment's strategic toolbox. The wiki's reading: if Goldwater is the political pivot, Phillips is the strategist who made the pivot durable.

Phillips's later trajectory was distinctive. By the 2000s he had become a sharp critic of the Republican Party, particularly of plutocratic concentration (*Wealth and Democracy*, 2002) and Christian-nationalist political theology (*American Theocracy*, 2006). The arc is wiki-noteworthy: the strategist of the 1960s realignment lived to denounce its 21st-century outcome.
