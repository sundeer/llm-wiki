# Newt Gingrich (b. 1943)

**Georgia Republican Congressman (1979–1999); Speaker of the House (1995–1999); architect of the 1994 "Contract with America" and the modern Republican confrontational-rhetoric register; the wiki's principal *transmission node* between [[1964 Republican National Convention|1964]]-Goldwater conservative temperament and [[2016 Election|2016]]-Trump conservative temperament.**

---

## Why He Matters Here

Per [[The Republican National Convention That Shocked the Country|Holden 2024]], Gingrich is the through-line figure for the wiki's *temperamental* continuity thesis between 1964 and 2016:

- **1978**: lectured young Republican activists that the party didn't do enough to "encourage you to be nasty."
- **1980s House**: regularly described Democrats as "pro-communist, anti-American, tyrannical."
- **1990 GOPAC memo**: provided the canonical word-list for Republicans to apply to Democrats — "sick, pathetic, lie, anti-flag, traitors, radical, corrupt." This is the wiki's clearest single artifact of the Republican Party's *deliberate* cultivation of the rhetorical register that became Trumpian thirty years later.
- **1994**: led the Republican House takeover; deployed the Contract with America as a national-platform mechanism.
- **1995**: Speaker of the House.
- **2016 onward**: enthusiastic Trump supporter; one of the few major Reagan-Bush-Bush-era Republicans who retained operational standing in MAGA.

Gingrich's structural significance for the wiki: he is the figure who made the *style* of conservative confrontation that Goldwater pioneered into the *standard operating mode* of Republican politics. The wiki's 1964→2016 continuity claim does not require positing a hidden lineage; the lineage runs through an entirely public political career.
