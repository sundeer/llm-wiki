# Clarence Thomas

**Supreme Court Associate Justice (1991–); the most consequential institutional bridge between Claremont's natural-right jurisprudence and the federal judiciary; cites the Declaration of Independence in his constitutional reasoning under direct Claremont influence**

As chairman of the EEOC under Reagan and George H.W. Bush, Thomas hired Claremont scholars **Ken Masugi** and **John Marini** as aides — theorists "who understood Lincoln," in his own description. During what Thomas later recalled as a period of "great intellectual development," they spent hours discussing [[Harry V. Jaffa]]'s teachings on natural law. Thomas began citing the Declaration of Independence in his jurisprudence — the signature move of [[Declarationism]] — and has remained the [[Claremont Institute]]'s most prominent ally on the bench.

The Claremont–Thomas connection is one of the wiki's clearest examples of how a marginal academic school (West Coast Straussianism) was institutionalized into the highest reaches of American power.
