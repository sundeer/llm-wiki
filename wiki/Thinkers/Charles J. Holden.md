# Charles J. Holden

**Professor of history at St. Mary's College of Maryland; co-author (with Zach Messitte and Jerald Podair) of *Republican Populist: Spiro Agnew and the Origins of Donald Trump's America* (University of Virginia Press, 2019); the wiki cites him as the author of [[The Republican National Convention That Shocked the Country]] (*TIME* "Made by History," July 17, 2024), the 60th-anniversary retrospective tracing the through-line from the 1964 Cow Palace convention to the contemporary GOP.**

The Holden-Messitte-Podair *Republican Populist* book is wiki-relevant beyond its TIME piece — it argues that **Spiro Agnew** is the under-recognized intermediate transmission node between Goldwater 1964 and Trump 2016, particularly for the rage-at-media and racially-coded-resentment registers. The book has not been ingested as a wiki source but is flagged as a candidate for future ingest.
