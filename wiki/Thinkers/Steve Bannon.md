# Steve Bannon

**Political strategist; former Goldman Sachs banker and Hollywood producer; executive chairman of Breitbart News; Trump's chief strategist (2017); associated with the [[Hard Right]] and economic nationalism; convicted of contempt of Congress (2022)**

---

## Who He Is

Steve Bannon is the most important political *operator* — rather than intellectual — of the MAGA New Right. Where the Claremonters and postliberals theorize, Bannon acts. His career spans finance (Goldman Sachs), entertainment (documentary films), right-wing media (Breitbart), and political strategy (Trump's 2016 campaign and transition).

He is not primarily a theorist but an ideological entrepreneur: someone who synthesizes ideas from multiple traditions and weaponizes them for political combat.

---

## Intellectual Influences

Bannon's self-described intellectual influences are eclectic and revealing:
- **Julius Evola** — Italian fascist esotericist; Bannon cited Evola approvingly in a Vatican speech
- **René Guénon** — French traditionalist philosopher
- **The Fourth Turning** (Strauss and Howe) — generational crisis theory; Bannon believes we are in a "Fourth Turning" civilizational crisis
- **Economic nationalism** — protectionist, anti-globalist, industrial policy

This synthesis places Bannon in the orbit of the [[Hard Right]]'s more esoteric currents — closer to [[Curtis Yarvin]] and the Bronze Age Pervert than to the Claremonters or Catholic postliberals, despite occasional overlaps.

---

## Strategic Role

Bannon's primary contributions to the New Right are strategic and organizational:
- **Breitbart News**: transformed it from a center-right blog into the internet's most influential MAGA media outlet
- **The "administrative deconstruction" doctrine**: Bannon's stated goal in 2017 was "deconstruction of the administrative state" — a strategic complement to Vermeule's [[Integration from Within]]
- **The [[Populism|populist]] electoral strategy**: Bannon understood before almost anyone else that working-class white voters without college degrees were a winnable coalition for economic nationalism + cultural grievance
- **International network**: Bannon worked with European populist-nationalist parties (Front National, Lega Nord, AfD) to build a global nationalist movement

---

## Significance

[[Furious Minds|Field]] positions Bannon in the Hard Right camp despite his elite educational background (Harvard MBA). His significance is as a *translator*: he can move ideas from Evola and Guénon and Yarvin into political messaging that resonates with Rust Belt voters. The intellectual sophistication and the populist brutalism are both real.
