# Jared Taylor

**Founder of *American Renaissance* (1990) and the New Century Foundation; the most polished long-running American white-nationalist intellectual; central node in the [[Hard Right]] ecosystem alongside [[Richard Spencer]] and the alt-right networks of the 2010s**

Taylor predates and outlasts the alt-right moment. *American Renaissance* has hosted "race-realist" conferences since the 1990s and maintained the appearance of academic respectability while advancing explicit white-identitarian politics. Taylor functions as a generational bridge between older paleoconservative racialism (e.g. [[Sam Francis]], who wrote for him) and the post-2010 online far right.

The mainstream New Right is officially separated from Taylor's project, but the boundary is porous: Hard Right figures who circulate among NatCon-adjacent venues often have *American Renaissance* in their intellectual genealogy.
