# Joshua Tait

**Conservative intellectual historian; Substack writer; Niskanen Center affiliated; argues the American Conservative Movement has always been functionally counterrevolutionary rather than genuinely Burkean**

Tait writes "Notes Toward a History of the Conservative Movement" on Substack. His essay "Conservatives or Counterrevolutionaries?" (August 2024) is the primary secondary source for the wiki's [[Counterrevolution]] concept page. He has also written on Burnham, Jaffa, Whittaker Chambers, and Nicolas Gómez Dávila.

See [[Conservatives or Counterrevolutionaries?]] for his key argument.
