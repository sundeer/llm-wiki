# David Brog

**President of the [[Edmund Burke Foundation]]; co-organizer of National Conservatism with [[Yoram Hazony]] and [[Christopher DeMuth]]; Princeton undergraduate, Harvard Law; former executive director of Christians United for Israel (CUFI)**

Brog brings Christian-Zionist organizational experience and donor networks to the [[National Conservatism]] project. Paul Gottfried, in his 2019 Mencken Club presidential address, sarcastically described NatCon as organized by "a Zionist philanthropist, David Brog, and an Israeli nationalist Yoram Hazony" — a paleocon swipe that reflects the recurring tension between the Hazony/Brog NatCon project (philosemitic, pro-Israel, Anglo-Jewish) and the older paleoconservative right (often Buchananite, restrictionist, suspicious of Israel and Jewish neoconservative influence).
