# Louis Menand (b. 1952)

**Harvard Anne T. and Robert M. Bass Professor of English; *New Yorker* staff writer (since 2001); intellectual historian; author of *The Metaphysical Club* (2001, Pulitzer Prize) and *The Free World* (2021); the wiki cites him as the author of [[He Knew He Was Right]] (*New Yorker*, March 2001), the canonical short essay-review of [[Rick Perlstein]]'s *Before the Storm* and a clean statement of the Goldwater-as-premonition thesis.**
